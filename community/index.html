CardCapture community terms

Version 1 · in force from the release of CardCapture 1.4

The community is the part of CardCapture where collectors follow each other and post about their cards. These terms are what you agree to when you make a profile there. They are in addition to the licence you use the app under (Apple's standard licence agreement). The rest of the app needs no profile and is not covered by this page.

1. Who can join

You must be 13 or older. If the law where you live asks for a parent's permission at your age, you need it. One person, one profile: the profile is yours and is tied to your Apple Account.

2. Your profile and what you post

What you post is yours. By posting it you allow CardCapture to show it to the people your profile's setting lets see it, inside the app, for as long as it is there. That is the only use made of it. You can delete a post, a comment or your whole profile at any time, and it is removed from the community at once.

A profile is private until you open it. What a profile shows of a collection is described in the privacy policy. Showing what a collection is worth is for members aged 18 and over; others see its change in percent only.

When you join, your profile follows CardCapture's own account, @cardcapture, which posts news about the app and about new sets. You can unfollow it at any time.

3. The rules

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. In particular:

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate.
  • Nothing sexual, and nothing that puts a child at risk.
  • No scams, no fraud, and no figures you made up.
  • No links, email addresses, phone numbers or usernames for other apps. The community has no private messages, and nobody should be moved somewhere else.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else, and do not use a name or username that suggests you speak for CardCapture or for a card's publisher.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that is not yours to post.
  • A profile photo shows you or something of yours: no other people, nothing sexual, no text or contact details, and nothing that shows your school, your home or your street.

4. Reporting and blocking

Every post, comment and profile can be reported from its menu, and anyone can be blocked. Blocking takes effect at once: you no longer see each other, and you stop following each other.

Reports are looked at within 24 hours. What breaks these terms is removed, and the person who posted it is told. A profile that keeps breaking them, or breaks them seriously, is paused or removed from the community. The person who reported is told what was decided; the person reported is not told who reported.

Some things are checked before they are posted: words the community does not allow, and links and contact details, are refused by the server. A profile photo is checked before anyone else sees it: first automatically, by an image model that Cloudflare runs for us and that neither keeps the photo nor learns from it, and by the developer when that check is not sure. A photo that is refused is not shown; you are told, and can write to us if you think that is wrong.

Anyone, member or not, can report content they believe is illegal by writing to support@cardcaptureapp.site. Material that puts a child at risk is removed at once and reported to the police.

If you think a decision about you is wrong, write to support@cardcaptureapp.site and it will be looked at again.

5. Figures are not advice

The percentages and values people show are their own, from their own collections, and are estimates. Nothing in the community is financial advice or an offer to buy or sell.

6. Ending it

You can delete your profile at any time, in the app, on your profile. We can pause or remove a profile that breaks these terms, and can close the community; if we close it, the profiles and everything in them are deleted.

7. Changes

If these terms change, the app shows the new rules and asks you to accept them before you post again.
