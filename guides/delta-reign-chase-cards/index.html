Why there are no prices on this page

A set this new has no settled prices yet. In the first weeks, more boxes are opened every day, early sales are few, and a card can be worth noticeably more or less next week than it is today. A number written here would be out of date before you read it.

So this page tells you which cards matter. For what yours is worth today, scan it: CardCapture gives a price combined from several markets, shows the range when they disagree, and lets you see where every figure came from.

Worth knowing: the Legendary Stadiums

Delta Reign brings a new kind of card: Stadiums that come in pairs and are played from the hand together. Each pair makes one picture when the two halves sit side by side, so collectors keep them as a set.

  • Legendary Pyroduct, 094/103 and 095/103
  • Legendary Summit, 096/103 and 097/103
  • Legendary Trench, 098/103 and 099/103

Pulled one? Do this first

  1. Sleeve it straight away, before it touches the table, and put the sleeve in a rigid holder.
  2. Check the number. Anything above 103 is a secret rare; the tables above say which one.
  3. Get today’s price, as a range from several markets, and check it again in a week or two. Early prices move.
  4. Think before you grade. Few graded copies have sold yet, so nobody knows what a 10 is worth. Read is my Pokémon card worth grading? before you send it in.

Questions

What is the rarest card in Delta Reign?

Mega Rayquaza ex, 141/103, the gold Mega Hyper Rare. It is the only card of that rarity in the set. The Mega Rayquaza ex Special Illustration Rare, 138/103, is the other headline card.

How many cards are in Delta Reign?

141: a main set of 103 cards and 38 secret rares numbered 104 to 141 (14 Illustration Rares, 17 Ultra Rares, 6 Special Illustration Rares and 1 Mega Hyper Rare).

When did Delta Reign come out?

The English set was released on 6 November 2026. Its Japanese counterpart, Storm Emeralda, came out on 31 July 2026.

How much are Delta Reign cards worth?

It changes week to week while the set is new, which is why this page lists no prices. Scan the card in CardCapture for today’s price, with the range and where it came from.
